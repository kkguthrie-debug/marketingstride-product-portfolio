# MarketingStride | AI Product & MarTech Portfolio

**Bringing together marketing, AI, product, customer experience, and technology to build and commercialize an AI-native SaaS platform.**

MarketingStride is an AI Findability platform designed to help organizations understand how AI systems interpret, evaluate, and surface their website content. It evaluates individual pages, websites, and competitive digital experiences using a consistent framework, turning complex signals into prioritized, actionable recommendations.

## The Product

MarketingStride evaluates how effectively digital content can be understood, supported, and surfaced by AI-driven discovery experiences.

The platform includes capabilities for:

* **Page Audits** to evaluate individual page signals and identify improvement opportunities
* **Website Audits** to establish a broader view across representative site content
* **Rescans and Comparisons** to measure improvement over time
* **Competitor Analysis** to evaluate comparable digital experiences using the same framework
* **Findings and Recommendations** that translate evaluation signals into prioritized actions
* **Executive and Detailed Reporting** designed for different levels of decision-making

The product is designed to move beyond a single score by showing what is affecting performance, why it matters, and what organizations can do next.
